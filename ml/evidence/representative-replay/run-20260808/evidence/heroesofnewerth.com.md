# Choose Your Role

##### Find your playstyle and shape every fight — from clutch saves to game-winning initiations.

![Carry icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/carry.png)

##### Carry

Farm early, dominate late. Lead your team to victory.

![Mid icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/mid.png)

##### Mid

Control tempo through early power spikes, rotations, and burst damage.

![Hard Support icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/hard_support.png)

##### Hard Support

Provide vision and protection, empower and sustain teammates.

![Soft Support icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/soft_support.png)

##### Soft Support

Roam early, securing kills and vision. Transitions to a playmaker.

![Offlane icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/offlane.png)

##### Offlane

Tough initiator, pressure lanes and create space.

![Jungle icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/role/jungle.png)

##### Jungle

Farm camps efficiently, gank lanes, and control jungle vision.

# Epic Bosses

##### Secure game-changing objectives and tilt the balance in your favor.

![Midwars boss icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/phoenix-icon.png)

### Phoenix

The Phoenix rises from every battle’s ashes, dropping special items and its coveted Egg.

- Resides opposite Kongor, across the river’s divide
- A scaling objective that grants powerful Phoenix items
- Slay it to earn the Phoenix Egg, resurrecting an ally upon death

![Kongor icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/kongor-icon.png)

### Kongor

Beneath the roar of the river lies Kongor, the worldbreaker. His fall releases devastation.

- Dwells across the river from the Phoenix
- Slay him to release the mighty Kongor summon
- Seizing Kongor turns the tide with an powerful lane push

# Choose Your Map

##### Two iconic experiences: classic tri-lane macro or nonstop mid-lane mayhem.

![Forests of Caldavar icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/maps/foc_icon.png)

#### Forests of Caldavar

The legendary battleground — tri-lane strategy, jungles, and epic boss fights.

- Tri-lane layout with jungles and rune control
- Objective play: towers, Phoenix, and Kongor
- Rewards coordination, rotations, and map awareness

![Midwars icon](https://heroesofnewerth.com/hon-content/media/img/maps/midwars_icon.png)

#### Midwars

Fast, chaotic, and endlessly fun — nonstop action in a single-lane battlefield.

- Single central lane — non-stop fighting
- Quick matches, heavy teamwork, constant aggression
- Perfect for casual and quick play
